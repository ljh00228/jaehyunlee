About

Research Background

I am a master's student at Hongik University's Nanomaterials Design Lab (Advisor: Prof. Won-kyu Lee), specializing in surface and interfacial science, nanoscale electrocatalyst design, and high-efficiency electrochemical energy-conversion devices.

My work investigates nanoscale structure–property relationships in water electrolysis systems via advanced spectroscopy and microscopy. I am currently developing ionomer-free catalyst architectures for high-efficiency and durable electrochemical energy conversion, and I also serve as an XPS instrument operator and analyst at Hongik University's Cooperative Center for Research Facilities.

I am actively seeking PhD positions in renewable energy, electrochemistry, and advanced materials science.
