About

Research Background

I am a master's student working on advanced catalytic materials for hydrogen generation and PEM water electrolysis. My research combines materials synthesis, plasma-based surface engineering, spectroscopy, and electrochemical characterization to understand structure–property relationships in low-dimensional materials.

I am currently seeking PhD positions at the intersection of renewable energy, advanced electrochemistry, and materials science, where I can contribute to the development of next-generation hydrogen production technologies.

Featured Projects

Selected Research Work

Strain-Engineered PtSe₂ Electrocatalyst for Hydrogen Evolution

01
  • Investigated low-dimensional PtSe₂ as a high-performance HER catalyst under controlled strain conditions
  • Combined experimental characterization (XPS, Raman, AFM, electrochemistry) with theoretical interpretation
  • Demonstrated enhanced catalytic activity through strain-mediated electronic structure modulation
ACS Nano · 2025

O₂ Plasma Activation of PtSe₂ Basal Planes

02
  • Investigated surface modification via O₂ plasma treatment for targeted defect engineering and catalytic activation
  • Characterized structural and chemical changes using XPS, Raman spectroscopy, and AFM
  • Correlated surface chemistry with electrochemical performance metrics (CV, Tafel, EIS)

MEA-Oriented Catalyst Layer Integration for PEM Water Electrolysis

03
  • Focused on catalyst transfer protocols, interface design, and charge transport optimization in membrane electrode assemblies
  • Addressed practical integration challenges for scaling 2D material catalysts toward device-level performance
  • Evaluated MEA-level electrochemical stability and efficiency under realistic operating conditions
